## Direction contract

THESIS: A server panel that reads like one calm page of instructions, not a dashboard. It refuses the category default of a grid of identical stat cards under a left rail; the task (connect, allow, check, clear) leads, numbers are quiet rows.

OWN-WORLD: The landing page's world, extended: ink #0a0a0a and bone #f3f0e8 grounds (system-chosen), one signal yellow #ffe500 spent only on the primary action, the current selection and focus. Flat colour, 1px hairlines, 16px radius on grouped regions, 10px radius bold 48px controls. Bricolage Grotesque for page headings only; Figtree for everything else; monospace only for addresses and tokens. Drawn SVG icons in one stroke.

STORY: The operator understands in seconds whether the server is healthy and open or private, copies the address for the apps, makes a token for a friend, and trusts that the dangerous buttons are clearly marked and ask before acting.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim top bar (brand, Advanced switch, theme, sign out) and four tabs. Home opens with what needs attention (if anything), then "Connect your apps" with the relay address large and one yellow Copy, then a quiet status list. Phone: single column, tabs scroll.

FORM: Code-led Operate surface; list-led rather than card-led; direction pinned whole to the landing page's world ("match the landing page"), seed: none (pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
